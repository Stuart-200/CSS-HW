Pizza

Pizza (Italian: ['pittsa']), Neapolitan: ['pittsa] is a savory dish of Italian origin consisting of a usually round, flattened base of leavened wheat-based dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often various other ingredients (such as anchovies, mushrooms, onions, olives, pineapple, meat, etc.), which is then baked at a high temperature, traditionally in a wood-fired oven. [1] A small pizza is sometimes called a pizzetta. A person who makes pizza is known as a pizzaiolo.

Baking

Baking (Old English: bacan, Latin: coquere) is a culinary method that involves cooking food by surrounding it with dry heat, typically in an oven. [1] It is a foundational technique in the preparation of breads, cakes, pastries, pies, and cookies, where dough or batter transforms under controlled heat into a firm, structured delight. Traditionally, baking relies on leavening agents such as yeast, baking powder, or beaten eggs to introduce air and lightness into the final product. A person who specializes in baking is called a baker, and a place where baking is done commercially is a bakery.
